Johor, Malaysia Data Center

Southeast Asia's next digital powerhouse.

Why Johor

Power, space, proximity, all in one

With DayOne, Johor becomes a launchpad for high-density workloads, regional expansion, and sustainable infrastructure at scale. 
The border state extends Singapore’s digital advantage with scalable land, power-ready sites, and direct connectivity. Positioned just across the border, it offers hyperscalers and enterprises the ability to grow at speed—without compromise. As Johor's largest LEED-certified data center campus, DayOne operates across two sites at Nusajaya Tech Park and Kempas Tech Park with direct connectivity to Singapore.

Our Presence

Laying the foundation for
scalable, cross-border infrastructure

DayOne is at the forefront of Johor’s transformation into a hyperscale data center powerhouse. With over 478 MW of operational capacity across two campuses—Nusajaya Tech Park and Kempas Tech Park — and more to come, we provide the infrastructure necessary for high-density workloads.

Our facilities are designed for rapid deployment, with scalability and sustainability at their core. Johor’s proximity to Singapore, combined with its cost-effective resources and supportive regulatory environment, enables DayOne to deliver low-latency, high-performance solutions that meet the evolving demands of the digital economy.

As Malaysia's leading operator of green data centers, our facilities combine renewable power, LEED certification, and rapid deployment at scale.
